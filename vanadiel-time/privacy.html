Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 July 2026

Vana'diel Time ("the app") is a companion clock and reminder tool for Final Fantasy XI. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

Vana'diel Time does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything the app does happens on your device. The app ships with no internet permission and makes no network requests of its own.

What the app stores on your device

The app saves a small amount of data locally so it can remember your settings between launches. This never leaves your device:

  • Your reminder choices — which events you've asked to be reminded about, your chosen lead times, and your selected delivery method.
  • App preferences — such as your play-time schedule, if you set one.

This information is stored in the app's private storage using the operating system's standard preferences mechanism. It is not readable by other apps and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device for upcoming in-game events. These are generated and delivered entirely by your device's operating system. No notification content is sent to us or to any third party.

Calendar access (optional)

The app offers an optional delivery mode that writes your chosen reminders into a calendar on your device, as an alternative to notifications:

  • In local calendar mode, the app creates a calendar named "Vana'diel Time" that stays on your device and never syncs.
  • In synced calendar mode, the app writes reminder events into a calendar you choose. If that calendar is set up on your device to sync with an online account (for example, a Google account), your device's operating system — not this app — handles that synchronization according to that account's own policies.

Calendar access is only requested if you turn on a calendar delivery mode, and is only used to add, update, and remove the app's own reminder events. The app does not read, collect, or transmit your other calendar entries.

Exact alarms

On Android, the app may request permission to schedule exact alarms so reminders arrive close to their intended time. This permission is used only for on-device reminder scheduling. If you decline it, reminders still work but may be delivered a little later.

Data sharing

We do not sell, share, or transmit any of your data. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking software.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
